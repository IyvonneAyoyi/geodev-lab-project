# Nairobi Flood Exposure

## Project Question

**Which settlements in Nairobi County sit in low-lying areas near rivers?**

## Project Description

This project will investigate potential flood exposure of settlements in Nairobi County by examining their location in relation to elevation and nearby rivers.

The analysis will identify settlements within Nairobi County, examine the elevation of the areas where they are located, and assess their proximity to rivers and other waterways. The results will help identify settlements that are potentially exposed based on these spatial characteristics.

The project will begin with publicly available geospatial datasets and will focus on developing a clear, reproducible spatial analysis.

## Study Area

**Nairobi County, Kenya**

## Data Sources

The project will use the following datasets:

### 1. GRID3 Settlement Extents

**Purpose:** To identify and map settlements within Nairobi County.

**Source:** GRID3
https://grid3.org/

### 2. Elevation Data

**Purpose:** To identify low-lying areas within the study area.

**Potential sources:**

* SRTM
* Copernicus DEM

### 3. Waterways

**Purpose:** To identify rivers and other waterways and assess their proximity to settlements.

**Source:** OpenStreetMap (OSM)
https://www.openstreetmap.org/

### 4. Administrative Boundary Data

**Purpose:** To define the Nairobi County study area and clip the analysis to the county boundary.

**Source:** An appropriate publicly available administrative boundary dataset for Kenya.

## Initial Analysis

The initial analysis will combine settlement, elevation, waterway, and administrative boundary data to investigate which settlements are located in low-lying areas near rivers.

The specific thresholds used to define **"low-lying"** and **"near rivers"** will be determined after examining the available datasets and considering an appropriate analytical approach.

## Expected Output

The project will produce a spatial analysis and map showing settlements in Nairobi County that are potentially exposed based on their elevation and proximity to rivers.
